KittenBytes

Retro Cats with Mega Purrsonalities

Hi! I'm Marta and I'm the creator behind Kittenbytes

I'm from Spain, currently re-training as a front-end developer after some years away from the field.

In December 2024 I decided to join the retro-themed Codedéx hackaton because I'm a big lover of all things old school (I'm an elder millennial, so I am a bit retro myself *cough*), and because after quite a few months of intense studying I wanted to do something strictly for fun. This really seemed the perfect occasion to do so, as well as a purrfect perfect way to close the year 💗

You have more details about the project on its github page, but I wanted to give due credit to all the pixel-tastic resources I've used to make this little site in less than 48 hours. Thank you so much!
